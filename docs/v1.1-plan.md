# IvanDAW v1.1 — Development plan

Design: `docs/v1.1-tech-design.md` (copy of the Claude Doc). Beatality's part is done in the Beatality repo from its own design; here it is only **Blocker B**.

How work lands (see "Repo workflow" in the design):

- ivan-daw and ivan-daw-core: commit straight to `main` after `pnpm check` is green. No PRs, no labels.
- **check** = after the push Claude writes in the session what to open, click and listen for, and waits for Ivan's "ok" before starting tasks that depend on it. Fixes go in follow-up commits.
- **checks-only** = Claude continues without waiting.
- **blocked by B** = waits until Ivan says Blocker B is cleared: the Beatality PR is merged, CI has published a `daw-build-<n>` release with `beatality-daw.zip`, and the PR has its "Ready for IvanDAW" section.

Localhost check, every time: `git pull`, `pnpm i`, `pnpm dev`, open http://localhost:5173.

## Listening checks per task

- **T2** Right-drag and two-finger scroll move all nodes and cables together; dragging a node and drawing a cable still land where the pointer is after panning; `+ Add` puts the new node in the middle of the view.
- **T3** Every audio node and Master show Volume and Pan on the box; Master pan moves the sine tone left and right without clicks.
- **T4** `/play_only` and `?play_only` show the "Play only" tag; Play, Stop, Space, BPM, volume and pan work; `+ Add`, Remove, Delete, node drag and cable drawing do nothing. Plain `/` is unchanged.
- **T6 + T7** Add Control sliders; draw from `a` to the sine synth's `ctl` input, pick Level: the slider changes the tone's level, and the panel shows "← Control sliders" with the Level slider disabled. Delete the cable: the level returns. Re-aim to Pan: the slider pans. Works in `/play_only` too.
- **T8** Draw LFO → sine synth Volume: the tone swells once per bar; raise BPM: it swells faster; Stop: it stays still.
- **T9** Replace note-gen with Note hold → sine synth: Play gives one steady C1 tone, Stop ends it cleanly; change Note while playing: the pitch jumps without a hanging tone.
- **T12** Beatality's own UI shows in its box; header drags it; picking a pack works; Play loops it from Note hold; Stop silences it; box volume and pan work.
- **T13** The design's full listening checklist, in `/` and `/play_only`.
